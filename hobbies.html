My Hobbies

  • 🏒

    Hockey

    I love playing hockey. It's a great workout and I like the team aspect of it. Whether I'm playing or just watching a game, it's always a good time. Plus it's taught me a lot about working with other people and not giving up when things get tough.

  • 🏈

    Minnesota Vikings

    I'm a huge Vikings fan. Watching games with my friends is one of my favorite things to do. Yeah, they can be frustrating sometimes, but that's part of being a fan. It's a good way to take a break from school and just hang out.

  • 🎯

    Outdoor Stuff

    Minnesota is great for being outside. I like hiking in the summer and doing winter stuff when it's cold. It's nice to get away from everything and just be outside. Plus it's a good way to clear my head when school gets stressful.
