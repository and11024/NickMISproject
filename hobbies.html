My Hobbies

  • 🏒

    Hockey

    Playing hockey is one of my greatest passions. The teamwork, strategy, and physical challenge combine to create an incredibly rewarding experience. Whether I'm on the ice or cheering from the stands, hockey teaches me valuable lessons about leadership, perseverance, and working toward a common goal.

  • 🏈

    Minnesota Vikings

    Being a Vikings fan is more than just following a team – it's about community and shared passion. Game days with friends create lasting memories and remind me that life is about more than just work and studies. The ups and downs of following a sports team also teach resilience and patience.

  • 🎯

    Outdoor Adventures

    Living in Minnesota provides incredible opportunities to explore the great outdoors. From hiking in state parks during summer to snowshoeing in winter, connecting with nature helps me clear my mind and gain perspective. These activities remind me of the importance of work-life balance.

  • 🎮

    Gaming & Technology

    I enjoy strategic games that challenge my thinking and problem-solving skills. This interest in gaming actually complements my finance studies, as both require analytical thinking, strategic planning, and understanding complex systems. Check out my custom-built game experience!
