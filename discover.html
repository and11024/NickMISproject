University of Minnesota Duluth

My experience so far at UMD

Going to UMD has been pretty cool so far. I've learned a lot and met some great people.

UMD is a good school and the people here are really nice. The classes are challenging but the professors actually care about helping you learn. Plus they try to make things practical, which I like.

The campus is right by Lake Superior which is awesome. It's a nice place to be and the facilities are pretty good. I feel like I have what I need to do well here.

UMD Highlights

  • 🎓

    Classes & Academics

    The Finance program here is solid. The professors know their stuff and they've actually worked in the real world, so they can tell you how things actually work. It's not just textbook stuff - they show you how to use what you're learning.

  • 🏛️

    Campus Life

    UMD is a smaller school so you actually get to know people. It's not super competitive - people help each other out. I've made some good friends here and the professors are pretty approachable if you need help.

  • 🌊

    Duluth & Location

    Duluth is a cool place to go to school. It's a college town but it's also right on the lake, so you get both. There's stuff to do in town and you can always go outside if you want. It's a good balance.

  • 🤝

    Getting Ready for After College

    UMD has career services that help you figure out what's next. They bring in people from different companies and help you network. It's nice to have that support when you're trying to figure out what you want to do.
