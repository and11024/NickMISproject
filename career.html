Professional Goals & Vision

My goals and what I'm working toward

I'm studying Finance at UMD and I'm trying to figure out what I want to do after I graduate.

I like finance because it's not just about numbers - it's about understanding how businesses actually work. I want to help companies make good decisions and find opportunities to grow.

Right now I'm just trying to learn as much as I can and get experience so I can be good at whatever I end up doing.

Career Objectives

  • 💼

    Financial Analysis & Strategy

    I want to get really good at analyzing financial stuff and helping companies make decisions. I'm interested in corporate finance, investment stuff, or maybe consulting. Basically anything where I can actually make a difference.

  • 📊

    Leadership & Working with Teams

    I want to get better at leading people and working with teams. I'll probably start by just doing my job well, then hopefully move into roles where I can help other people and work with bigger groups. I think it's important to help others learn and grow.

  • 🎯

    Finding My Niche

    I want to get really good at something specific. I'm interested in industries that are changing or growing a lot - those seem like they'd be interesting to work in. I'm still figuring out exactly what that might be though.

  • 🌟

    Keep Learning

    I know I'll need to keep learning even after I graduate. I might get some certifications like CFA or CPA depending on what I end up doing. I just want to stay on top of things and keep getting better.
